BLOCK 5 — THE AIF GAP-START RE-WALK (proposal's "fold it into step 2 or 3").

`passes.rs` emits `run_aif`'s accepted gap starts straight to the commit stream
without the walk re-seed Stages 2 and 3 do. Step 2 predicted that closing it IS a
walk re-seed and left it here. Measured on this branch, with a temporary re-seed
after `run_aif` (reverted; not in this diff), over the same 96 images:

  tailcallentry OFF: recall 40309 -> 40396 (+87); new 1355 (gt 174, splits 1013,
                     outside 168); LOST 109 entries, 87 of them ground truth
  tailcallentry ON:  recall 40870 -> 40956 (+86); new 1424 (gt 193, splits 1059,
                     outside 172); LOST 135 entries, 107 of them ground truth

NOT folded in, and the tail-call split does not make it safe — it makes it
slightly worse. The structural reason is the same one that makes this PR additive:
a tail-call split moves a target from the walk's instruction worklist to its
function worklist, which leaves the decoded-instruction closure fixed. A re-seed
ADDS instructions to that closure, which turns undefined gaps into code and makes
other stages' candidates fail their `is_undefined` guard — so entries that were
being emitted stop being emitted. Nothing about tail calls can defend against
that. It needs its own investigation.
