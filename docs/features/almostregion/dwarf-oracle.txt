Verdict: every reported member block lies inside DWARF-confirmed inlined
code.  Inlining here is NESTED - build_spec_list spans 0x434d-0x49b7, i.e.
most of the function, and the others sit inside it - so the meaningful unit
is the INNERMOST callee covering a block.  By that reading the first region
is a clean single-callee hit (append_char_class) and the second spans two
adjacent innermost callees (append_equiv_class and append_char_class), a
real imprecision that is not claimed otherwise.

Note also the high prior: build_spec_list alone covers most of parse_str, so
"lands inside inlined code" is weak evidence on THIS function by itself.  The
discriminating measurement is the corpus-level one in analysis.md, not this
single pane.

NOTE the members are a block SET, not an address interval: the exits sit at
LOWER addresses than the heads.  An inlined callee is laid out in several
disjoint pieces - DWARF records 3 to 6 ranges for these callees - so any
consumer must take the member list and never head..exit.

Counter-example, stated because it bounds the claim:
  openssh-portable/ssh::sshkey_drop_cert @ O2 0x42c50 has three
  DWARF-confirmed inlined callees (sshkey_type_is_cert, cert_free,
  sshkey_type_plain) and ZERO gotos even with every goto-removal pass
  disabled.  No goto means no virtualized edge means nothing to probe.
  almostregion finds the messy half of inlining, by construction.
